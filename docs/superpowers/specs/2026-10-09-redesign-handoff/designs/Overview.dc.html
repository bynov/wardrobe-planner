1Walk-in Planner redesign: landing, app layouts, screens, mobile
System
Warm paper neutrals with one oak/terracotta accent. Schibsted Grotesk for UI, IBM Plex Mono for every dimension, so numbers line up and look measured. Drawings use the same tokens as the UI, so light and dark both work. The theme follows the system by default, and the toggle cycles Auto → Light → Dark.
What changed vs. the MVP
Secondary actions (Import/Export JSON, language) move into ⋯, leaving Share and Export PDF visible. Room settings collapse to one-line summaries. Units are inserted from a preset tray with mini diagrams. The inspector shows zones as a visual stack, and only the selected zone expands. Validation shows as one clear banner, and the PDF button is disabled until it's fixed. Everything is clickable. Undo works.
