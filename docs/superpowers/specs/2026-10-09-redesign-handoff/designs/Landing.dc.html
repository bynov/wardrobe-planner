Three steps from empty room to drawings.

The planner does the arithmetic: corner claims, door clearance, ceiling gap and plinth. You decide what goes where.

01Type in the roomWidth, depth and height, then where the door is and which way it swings.
02Pick the wallsBuild against one, two or three walls. Free length is shown as you go.
03Stack the columnsHanging, shelves, drawers and shoe racks, set zone by zone inside each unit.

What it doesn’t do

It plans an open walk-in: carcasses, shelves, dividers, drawer fronts and hanging rods. If you need hinged fronts, runner specs or an odd-shaped room, this is the wrong tool.
