What it doesn’t do

It plans an open walk-in: carcasses, shelves, dividers, drawer fronts and hanging rods. If you need hinged fronts, runner specs or an odd-shaped room, this is the wrong tool.

No doors on unitsFronts are drawer fronts only. Units are open.
No drawer boxes or hardwareRunners, hinges and brackets are left to you.
Rectangular rooms onlyFour straight walls, one door.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Walk-in Planner is free and open source under the MIT licence. There is no account, no sign-up and no paid tier.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern desktop or mobile browser. There is nothing to download and no plugin to install.

Where is my project stored?

In your browser's localStorage, on the device you are working on. Nothing is uploaded to a server. Export the project as a JSON file to back it up or move it to another machine, and import that file to carry on.

What does the cut list include?

Every carcass panel — sides, top, bottom, back and plinth — plus shelves, dividers, drawer fronts and hanging rods, with sizes and quantities, tagged (B1, R2, L1) to match the drawings. Drawer boxes, runners and other hardware are not included.

Can I work in inches?

Yes. A units toggle switches the whole interface, drawings and cut list between millimetres and inches. The model itself stays in millimetres, so numbers never drift as you switch back and forth.

What can I export?

A PDF containing the dimensioned plan, one elevation per wall and the cut list — enough to take to a panel shop — and the project itself as a JSON file you can re-import later.
